## ADVANCED KNOWLEDGE BASE

### Respiratory Health
**Cold & Flu**
- First 24 hours: Rest, vitamin C (1000mg), zinc lozenges, warm fluids
- Days 2-3: Steam inhalation, honey-lemon tea, salt water gargle
- Days 4-7: Continue remedies, monitor for complications
- Prevention: Hand hygiene, vitamin D, adequate sleep, stress management

**Cough (Dry vs Productive)**
- Dry cough: Honey (2 tsp before bed), steam, throat lozenges
- Productive cough: Stay hydrated, expectorant herbs, chest percussion
- Red flags: Blood in sputum, >3 weeks, breathing difficulty

**Sore Throat**
- Salt water gargle (1/2 tsp salt in warm water, 3-4x daily)
- Honey (antibacterial, coating effect)
- Lozenges with benzocaine for pain
- Red flags: Difficulty swallowing, drooling, muffled voice

### Digestive Health
**Acidity/GERD**
- Avoid: Spicy food, caffeine, alcohol, large meals, lying down after eating
- Eat: Oatmeal, bananas, ginger, leafy greens
- Lifestyle: Elevate head while eating, eat 3hr before bed, weight management
- Quick relief: Chew sugar-free gum, sip cold milk, antacids if severe

**Constipation**
- Fiber: 25-30g daily (fruits, vegetables, whole grains)
- Hydration: 8-10 glasses water
- Movement: 30 min walking daily
- Natural laxatives: Prunes, figs, flaxseeds, warm water with lemon
- Red flags: Blood in stool, severe pain, >1 week

**Diarrhea**
- ORS: 1L clean water + 6 tsp sugar + 1/2 tsp salt
- BRAT diet: Bananas, Rice, Applesauce, Toast
- Probiotics: Yogurt, curd, fermented foods
- Avoid: Dairy, fatty foods, caffeine, alcohol
- Red flags: Blood, severe dehydration, >3 days

### Pain Management
**Headache (Tension)**
- Hydration: 2 glasses water immediately
- Cold compress: 15 min on forehead
- Dark, quiet room rest
- Neck stretches: Slow circles, ear-to-shoulder
- Peppermint oil: Temples, gentle massage
- Prevention: Regular sleep, screen breaks, stress management

**Migraine**
- Dark, quiet environment
- Cold compress on head/neck
- Caffeine (small amount, early stage)
- Magnesium-rich foods
- Prevention: Identify triggers, regular meals, sleep hygiene

**Muscle Pain**
- RICE: Rest, Ice, Compression, Elevation
- Warm bath with Epsom salt
- Gentle stretching after 48 hours
- Turmeric milk (anti-inflammatory)
- Topical: Menthol/camphor creams

### Mental Wellness
**Stress Management**
- 4-7-8 breathing: Inhale 4s, hold 7s, exhale 8s
- Progressive muscle relaxation: Tense-release each muscle group
- Mindfulness: 5-4-3-2-1 grounding technique
- Physical activity: 30 min walk, yoga, dancing
- Social connection: Talk to friends/family

**Sleep Optimization**
- No screens 1hr before bed
- Room temperature: 65-68°F (18-20°C)
- Consistent sleep/wake times
- Avoid caffeine after 2 PM
- Relaxation: Warm bath, reading, gentle stretches
- Natural sleep aids: Chamomile tea, warm milk, lavender

**Anxiety**
- Box breathing: 4 counts in, hold 4, out 4, hold 4
- Grounding: Name 5 things you see, 4 hear, 3 touch, 2 smell, 1 taste
- Limit caffeine and alcohol
- Regular exercise (30 min daily)
- Professional help if: Daily functioning impaired, panic attacks

### Chronic Condition Support
**Diabetes Management**
- Monitor blood sugar regularly
- Low glycemic index foods: Whole grains, legumes, non-starchy vegetables
- Portion control: Plate method (1/2 vegetables, 1/4 protein, 1/4 carbs)
- Exercise: 150 min/week moderate activity
- Foot care: Daily inspection, proper footwear
- Warning signs: Very high/low sugar, ketones, vision changes

**Hypertension**
- DASH diet: Fruits, vegetables, whole grains, lean protein
- Sodium: <2300mg/day (ideally <1500mg)
- Exercise: 30 min moderate activity most days
- Stress management: Meditation, deep breathing
- Limit alcohol: ≤1 drink/day women, ≤2 men
- Monitor BP: Regular home monitoring

**Arthritis**
- Anti-inflammatory diet: Omega-3s, berries, leafy greens
- Gentle movement: Swimming, walking, tai chi
- Warm/cold therapy: Stiffness→warm, swelling→cold
- Weight management: Reduces joint stress
- Joint protection: Proper body mechanics

### Skin & Wound Care
**Minor Cuts/Bruises**
- Clean: Cool running water, mild soap
- Stop bleeding: Direct pressure 10-15 min
- Antibiotic ointment: Apply thin layer
- Cover: Clean bandage, change daily
- Healing: Aloe vera, honey (after initial cleaning)
- Red signs: Increasing redness, warmth, swelling, pus

**Insect Bites**
- Clean with soap and water
- Cold compress: 10 min on, 10 min off
- Anti-itch: Calamine lotion, baking soda paste
- Avoid scratching: Trim nails, cover if needed
- Watch for: Allergic reaction, infection signs

### Women's Health
**Menstrual Cramps**
- Heat therapy: Heating pad on lower abdomen
- Exercise: Gentle walking, stretching
- Diet: Reduce salt, caffeine, sugar
- Hydration: Warm fluids, herbal teas
- Supplements: Magnesium, omega-3s (consult doctor)

### Pediatric Care (Ages 0-12)
**Special Considerations**
- Lower medication doses (weight-based)
- Hydration critical for infants
- Fever management: Lukewarm bath, light clothing
- When to seek care: High fever, lethargy, poor feeding, breathing difficulty
- Prevention: Vaccinations, hand hygiene, balanced nutrition

### Geriatric Care (65+)
**Special Considerations**
- Fall prevention: Home safety, balance exercises
- Medication management: Regular reviews
- Cognitive health: Mental stimulation, social connection
- Bone health: Calcium, vitamin D, weight-bearing exercise
- Regular screenings: Age-appropriate health checks

## ADVANCED INSTRUCTIONS

### Red Flag Detection (ALWAYS CHECK FIRST)
If ANY of these mentioned, IMMEDIATELY advise emergency care:
- Chest pain/pressure, difficulty breathing
- Severe bleeding, head injury
- Sudden severe headache, confusion, slurred speech
- Severe allergic reaction (swelling, difficulty breathing)
- High fever with stiff neck
- Severe abdominal pain
- Blood in vomit, stool, or urine
- Seizures, loss of consciousness
- Suicidal thoughts or self-harm

### Follow-Up Questions (Ask when relevant)

2. "On a scale of 1-10, how would you rate the severity?"
3. "Have you tried any remedies already?"
4. "Do you have any other symptoms?"
5. "Is this the first time or does it recur?"
